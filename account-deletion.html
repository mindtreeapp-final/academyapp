Delete Your Account

How to delete your Mindtree Academy account and data

On this page

  4. What we keep, and for how long

This page explains how to delete your Mindtree Academy account and the data linked to it. It applies to the Mindtree Academy apps for Android and iPhone and the Mindtree Academy website, all operated by Olivet Management Private Limited.

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted it cannot be recovered. You lose access to your courses, progress and certificates, and you are signed out on every device.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Mindtree Academy appSign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to your ProfileThen open Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap “Delete my account”Read the confirmation and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Profile → Settings → Account → Delete my account

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, fill in the deletion request form below. Please use the email address you registered with, so we can confirm the account is yours.

Form not loading? Open the deletion request form or email databasemindtree@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request.

We may reply to confirm the request came from you. We complete deletion requests within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, password hash and sign-in sessions. You are signed out on every device.
  • Your profile: name, country, state, qualification level, target exam and bio.
  • Your learning history: lessons opened, video and reading progress, completed items, practice answers and scores, mock-test attempts and results, bookmarks, revision list and wishlist.
  • Your certificates and their verification records.
  • Your devices: the list of devices signed in to your account.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Orders and invoices: kept after deletion, with your personal details removed, because Indian tax law requires us to keep records of sales.
  • Server logs (IP address and request details): kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

Deleting your account does not by itself give you a refund, unless the law says otherwise. See our Terms of Use.

Deleting only some of your data

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove some data. You can change or clear your profile details at any time in the app. To ask us to delete specific data, such as a question you sent to faculty, email databasemindtree@gmail.com from your registered address and tell us what you want removed.

Need help?

Email databasemindtree@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 97782 86707 (Monday to Saturday, 9am–6pm IST). For more on how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.
